# Doc of Lib Weixin

This lib provides abilities of Weixin Open Platform, covers access token and error handling.

## WXMP

`WXMP` refers to `微信公众平台`, this platform offers service account, miniprogram ability of Weixin.
Therefore, `WXMP_MP` refers to miniprogram on Weixin and `WXMP_SA` refers to service account on Weixin.
